# Community conduct

Be respectful and constructive. No harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, credential disclosure or personal attacks. Critique work, not people; respect licenses and provenance. Keep discussions relevant and preserve privacy.

The maintainer may moderate/remove unsafe material and restrict abusive participation. Use a verified private maintainer channel for sensitive concerns; no public disclosure of personal data. A full standardized policy/contact can be adopted before public community launch; this document does not claim affiliation with another policy author.
